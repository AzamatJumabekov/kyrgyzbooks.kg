Future Plans / Get Involved

We are in the process of forming a Kyrgyz Children's Book Industry Association, bringing together publishers, booksellers, authors, translators, and illustrators — focused on internationalizing the local publishing sector, building rights-management and translation capacity, and centering inclusivity and gender equity. The association will be open to secular content creators.

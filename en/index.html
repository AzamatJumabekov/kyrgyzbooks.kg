Storybooks that carry the melodies of Kyrgyz instruments and the sounds of Kyrgyzstan's animals to the world

We are a small Kyrgyz children's-book publisher. In 2026 we launched two titles — Animals of Kyrgyzstan and Musical Instruments of the Kyrgyz People — each one built around the sounds, stories, and craftsmanship of Kyrgyz culture.
